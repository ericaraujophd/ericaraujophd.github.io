# VerseNet: A Visual Neo4j-Based Bible Knowledge Network

**Students:** Martin Liu
**Supervisor:** Eric Araújo
**Term:** Spring 2026 to Fall 2026
**Status:** In progress

## Abstract

Theologians have spent the past millenia trying to understand the Bible, its historical figures, the relations between books, etc. These task has been enhanced quite fastly with currenct advancements in Computer Science and Mathematics. The complex systems area, more specifically, has changed the way we model the world mathematically, bring graphs as a way of organizing reality into theoretical (and now computational) models. The study of graphs considers that nodes and edges are the main actors in the way reality presents itself. The nodes for this project would be the 31,102 verses present in a protestant Bible, while new relationships deriving from them would be built in order to enrich the study of Scripture.

The change in the paradigm from a linear or 2 dimensional crossover between verses to a complex network requires tools that converse well with the new model. For that reason, we will use Neo4j to store the bible verses and work from there to create new edges based on factors sush as the sentiment, style, main actor of the verses, the presence of similar phrasal structures, etc. The work involves (1) setting up a Neo4j dataset based on the viz.Bible project ([viz.bible](https://viz.bible/)), (2) get acquainted and skilled on how Neo4j works, being able to perform queries, insertions, deletions, etc., (3) create a web interface that perform basic queries Neo4j and presents a friendly account of the results to the user, (4) provides an AI agent to develop more advanced queries, (5) and presents a reflection from the student point of view regarding outcomes and new features added to the database.

This tool could be used by theologians and lay people to develop further their investigations on the Bible.
