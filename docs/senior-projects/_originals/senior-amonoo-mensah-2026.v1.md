# What is AI talking about Junia?

**Students:** Sally Amonoo-Mensah
**Supervisor:** Eric Araújo
**Term:** Fall 2026 to Spring 2027
**Status:** In progress

## Abstract

Junia is considered by some theologians as the first official apostle after Jesus' ressurrection. We have not much but one Bible verse about this important figure within the emerging church, in Romans 16:7. The lack of documentation and more information about this figure has been a fertile ground for many theories, and affected the discussion of Women's ministry and importance in the first two centuries of the Christian church.

This project aims to evaluate directly and indirectly what AI responds when questions are raised about this woman. Directly, we can assess e.g. what bias, accuracy, historic relevance AI agentic tools such as ChatGPT and Claude models give when asked difficult, contraversial, or even simple questions about Junia. Indirectly, we know that much of the context AI takes into accound has documents such as books, articles, and other materials that are part of the corpus of studies developed in the past history that survived to be part of the digital age. That is to say that by prompting AI, we may also have glimpses of what scholars have also discussed and settled about Junia.

The main goal of this project is to evaluate 5 or more AI models by (1) studying and becoming an expert about the life of Junia according to what scholars have already discussed about her, (2) develop a question bank for Junia, by deciding which questions could provide rich information of how AI sees her, (3) evaluate the models, collect data, and use the rubric from the Matristics project to rank Junia according to historical accuracy, coverage, framing and agency, and epistemic quality, (4) review the rubric and the questions and add Junia to the Matristics project benchmark.
