COR INQUIETUM The Restless Heart in a Digital Age

Our heart is restless until it rests in you.

Augustine, Confessions I.1. This semester we read that sentence beside the research on phones and artificial intelligence, learn the four cardinal virtues, and live a common rule, one habit at a time. You finish by writing a rule of life for the years ahead.

01 · THE RHYTHM

How a class goes

SESSION 1 · 100 MINUTES

The sources

  • 30 min · Practice together. The week's discipline from Calhoun's handbook, such as silence or the examen.
  • 70 min · Seminar. A passage of Scripture read slowly, then Pieper's chapter, the questions from Aquinas, and Augustine or Lewis.
SESSION 2 · 100 MINUTES

The technology

  • 30 min · Practice together.
  • 15 min · The case. A student pair introduces the week's technology, most weeks an AI system, such as a chatbot, a recommendation algorithm or an AI companion.
  • 45 min · Discussion. The technology in the light of the week's virtue.
  • 10 min · Write. A note in your journal, by hand or on a distraction-free writing device.

Every two weeks, a unit

REPEATS SEVEN TIMES
  1. 1First week of the unit, both sessions
  2. 2Second week, both sessions again
  3. 3Name, aloud, the habits you are adding
  4. 4Keep the growing rule for two weeks, in your notebook and journal
  5. 5The next unit opens by asking how it went

The rule, one habit at a time

Outside class you keep the eight habits of Justin Whitmel Earley's The Common Rule. You begin in week 1 with Scripture before phone. Each unit adds the habits of its virtue and keeps the earlier ones, so by week 13 you live the whole rule. In the last unit you rewrite it as your own rule of life.

EVERY DAY

The notebook

A paper notebook you carry everywhere. Record the day as it happens, including your time on the phone, what you watched and for how long, what you used AI for, and which habits you kept.

EVERY WEEK

The journal

300 to 400 words by hand, scanned and submitted on the course site. Four prompts each week:

  1. What did I keep, and what did I miss?
  2. Where did I notice God's grace (or, if you are not a Christian, gift)?
  3. Where did I feel pulled toward the phone or AI, and what was I looking for?
  4. What does this week's virtue look like in my life right now?

03 · THE WORK

Assignments

This course uses mastery grading. Your work is assessed against six competencies, and each one is marked Mastered or Not yet. You may revise each essay once, after we talk about it. Mastery is judged on the depth of your thinking and reflection, never on how many habits you kept.

1VirtueYou explain the cardinal virtues as Augustine, Aquinas and Pieper describe them, and use them to read your own habits.SHOWN IN · ESSAYS 1 AND 2
2AnalysisYou show how a specific AI or digital system shapes attention, desire, trust and relationship.SHOWN IN · TECHNOLOGY SESSION AND ESSAY 3
3JudgmentYou evaluate a decision about designing or using technology in light of the DELTA principles, in clear, sourced writing.SHOWN IN · ESSAY 3
4ReflectionYou reflect in depth on the practices and the rule, with specific moments from your notebook, in at least 11 of the 14 weekly journal entries.SHOWN IN · WEEKLY JOURNAL
5Rule of lifeYour rule draws on the semester's practice and sources and includes at least one practice you designed, with how it works and what it aims to achieve.SHOWN IN · RULE OF LIFE
6VocationYou name what you would refuse to build or to use, and why.SHOWN IN · RULE OF LIFE AND ESSAY 3

THE WORK

Three essays of 1,000 to 1,200 words, in weeks 4, 8 and 12. One technology session, with a partner. A journal entry every week. Your rule of life, drafted in week 13 and finished in week 14.

A journal entry meets the standard when it answers all four prompts with specific moments from your notebook. Entries are not revised, which is why three may fall short.

THE FINAL PRODUCT

Your rule of life

One or two pages for the years ahead. It starts from the Common Rule you kept this semester and makes it your own, with a line or short paragraph for each cardinal virtue. It also includes at least one practice you design yourself, with how it works and what you aim to achieve. Each line names a concrete practice and the reason for it, and covers the technologies you use and the ones you may build. You present it, or the part you choose to share, at the public evening. It is judged on thoughtfulness, use of the sources and honesty about your own life, and never on what you commit to.

05 · THE ROOM

Phones, writing and AI

Phones stay away

In-class writing happens in your journal or on the classroom's distraction-free writing devices, so nothing on a screen competes for your attention.

AI in your writing

Your essays and your rule of life are in your own words. If you use an AI tool to explore a question, say so in a note at the end.
